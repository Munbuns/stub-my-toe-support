Privacy Policy

Stub My Toe is a local-first personal stub-free day tracker developed by Joseph Munford. This policy describes version 1.0 of the iPhone app, its Home Screen widgets, Apple Watch companion and Watch widgets. Effective November 2, 2026.

Tracking history and widgets

Your tracking start date, recorded stub dates and identifiers, streak information, and local app state are stored on your devices. The iPhone app and its widgets share the same local store through an Apple App Group. The developer does not receive your tracking history through the app.

Apple Watch synchronization

The companion saves a local incident queue on your Watch. Apple's WatchConnectivity framework sends incidents and acknowledgements between your paired devices and sends a streak summary from iPhone to Watch. Watch widgets read the Watch's local state. No developer server participates in this synchronization. Delivery may be delayed while either device is unavailable or while the operating system schedules background work.

No accounts, analytics or advertising

Stub My Toe has no developer account, developer-operated cloud service, analytics SDK or advertising SDK. It does not sell your tracking data or use it for advertising or cross-app tracking. Version 1.0 is free and has no subscriptions or in-app purchases.

Data retention and removal

The app does not automatically clear your history. You can briefly undo a newly recorded iPhone incident or remove an incident from iPhone History; streaks then recalculate. Queued Watch removals require confirmation from iPhone. Local synchronization identifiers and receipts may remain after a visible incident is removed, to prevent a repeated or previously removed Watch incident from being added again.

Removing the app can remove its local history. The iPhone and Watch keep their own local data; remove the app from both devices if you want to remove both local stores. Device backups may retain data according to your own Apple device and backup settings. Version 1.0 has no export/import or developer-operated account recovery feature.

Apple and GitHub services

Apple manages the App Store, TestFlight, device backup and paired-device services under its own terms and privacy practices. This support website and its public issue tracker are hosted by GitHub. Visiting the website or opening a support issue involves GitHub's services, separate from the app's local tracking. The developer can see information you choose to include in a public support issue; other people can see it too.

Your choices and contact

You control whether to record or remove an incident and whether to use the Watch companion. For app or privacy questions, contact Stub My Toe support through GitHub. A GitHub account is required to open an issue. Do not post sensitive tracking history, personal information, device identifiers or passwords in a public issue.
