Privacy policy

Stub My Toe: The Game

This policy applies to the Pip platformer by Joseph Munford, bundle identifier com.munbuns.stubmytoe.game.

Your game stays on your device

The game stores level unlocks, scores, lifetime play statistics, achievements, tutorial progress, and settings locally on your iPhone. There is no developer-operated game account, analytics server, advertising network, or tracking SDK. The developer does not receive your local save during normal play. Local game data is not sold or used for advertising.

Optional Game Center

If you choose to connect, Apple’s Game Center manages authentication and its online gaming features. The app may keep an app-scoped player identifier and display name on your device, and send eligible scores or achievement progress to Apple for supported modes. It does not send this information to a developer-operated server. Availability depends on the mode and Game Center configuration. Apple processes its service data under Game Center & Privacy and Apple’s Privacy Policy. Connecting is not required for local play.

No sensitive permissions or tracking

The game does not access your contacts, location, photos, camera, or microphone. It does not track you across apps or websites. It has no ads or in-app purchases.

Support and beta feedback

If you contact support, the developer receives what you choose to send, such as your email address, message, and attached screenshots. This information is used to answer your request and investigate issues, not for advertising. Please avoid including private information. You may request deletion of support correspondence by emailing the address below, subject to any necessary legal retention.

Apple’s TestFlight service may provide crash reports, testing information, and feedback according to your TestFlight choices and Apple’s terms. Apple controls this service; the game does not add a separate analytics or crash-reporting SDK.

Managing your data

You can change sound and comfort settings within the game. Disconnecting Game Center or managing its account is handled through Apple’s settings. Deleting the app may remove its local progress and settings; device backups are controlled by your device and Apple. Game Center information is managed through Apple’s services. We cannot restore a deleted local save from a developer server because no such server is operated.

This website

This support site is hosted by GitHub Pages and uses no custom analytics, advertising scripts, or cookies. GitHub may process standard web-request information under GitHub’s Privacy Statement. Opening external links is optional.
