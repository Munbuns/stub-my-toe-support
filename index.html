Stub My Toe Support

Track the days since your last stub, record an ouch, and cheer on your personal best with Pip.

Get help

For questions, bug reports, or feature requests, open a support request on GitHub. GitHub issues are public and require a GitHub account. Please do not include private tracking history, personal information, device identifiers, or passwords.

Quick help

Start tracking: Open Stub My Toe on iPhone and tap Start tracking. This establishes your starting date without recording a stub.

Record a stub: Tap OUCH! on Home. Your next streak starts from that incident. An immediate Undo is available briefly; you can also open History, choose the incident's remove button, and confirm. Your streaks recalculate from the remaining history.

See your records: Hall of Fame shows your current streak, personal best and top ten completed streaks. Streaks count calendar days using your device's local calendar and time zone, not complete 24-hour periods.

Home Screen widgets: Edit your iPhone Home Screen, find Stub My Toe in the widget gallery, and choose a small or medium widget. Its OUCH! action records a stub without opening the app. iOS manages refresh timing, so a widget may not update immediately. If history is unavailable, open the app.

Apple Watch: Start tracking on iPhone first, then open the companion on your paired Apple Watch. Tap OUCH! to save an incident on Watch. It stays queued until iPhone confirms it. The devices must be able to communicate for synchronization, which may be delayed in the background. Details shows the queue and synchronization status; Retry sync requests another attempt. Watch widgets open the companion app rather than recording directly.

Accidental Watch incident: Remove a confirmed incident in iPhone History. For queued incidents, use Details on Watch and request removal. Removal stays pending until iPhone confirms it, including an incident that may already have reached the phone.

Keep your history: Phone tracking works offline. The app stores history locally and has no developer-operated account, cloud backup, or export/import feature. Do not delete and reinstall the app as a troubleshooting step: removing it can remove local data. Keep the same app installed when updating.

Included features: Version 1.0 is free, with no ads, subscriptions or in-app purchases. Stub My Toe is a playful personal counter, not a medical app or the separate platformer game.
